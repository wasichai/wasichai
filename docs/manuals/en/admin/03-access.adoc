[[admin-access]]
== Users, roles and permissions

Common requirement: `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION` (or `ADMIN`). A service account can never use these screens.

[[admin-users]]
=== Users

menu:Administration[Users] has the *New user* form at the top and below it the list of people with their name,
email, roles, status and creation date (<<admin-fig-users>>). Your own row carries the mark _This is you_.

:admin-users-alt: New user form and the list with four people and their roles ADMIN, CONSULTA, REGISTRADOR and \
REVISOR
[[admin-fig-users]]
.Users of the example
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/06-users.png["{admin-users-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Objective
Add a person.

.Prerequisites
Their email, their name, an initial password that meets the policy of the organization (by default, at least 8
characters) and the roles they need.

.Procedure
. Fill in *Email*, *Name* and *Password*.
. Check their *Roles*.
. Click *Create*.

.Expected result
The person appears in the list as *Active* and can sign in.

.Verification
Ask the person to sign in; their name appears at the foot of the menu.

.Special cases
* An email repeated in the organization is rejected.
* A password that does not meet the policy is rejected with the reason (`Password too short` or
  `Password does not meet the password policy`).
* The initial password is passed on through a secure channel; the person cannot change it on their own from the
  interface.

Edit, disable or delete::
The pencil opens the edit form: name, *New password* (leave it empty to keep the current one), roles and status.
Disabling prevents signing in and keeps the account and its trail. You cannot disable or delete yourself.

CAUTION: With `wasichai.security.jwt.revocation=true`, disabling a person, changing their password or their roles,
or deleting them invalidates at once the credentials they already had. Without that option, the issued credentials
stay valid until they expire (8 hours by default). To withdraw access immediately, ask the technical team to turn
on revocation.

WARNING: Deleting a person cannot be undone. Prefer disabling them: their history stays readable.

[[admin-roles]]
=== Roles

menu:Administration[Roles] creates roles and shows how many permissions and restricted fields each one has
(<<admin-fig-roles>>).

:admin-roles-alt: New role form and the list with Administrator, Consulta, Registrador de licencias and Revisor \
técnico
[[admin-fig-roles]]
.Roles of the example
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/07-roles.png["{admin-roles-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Procedure
. Type the *Technical name* in upper case (`INSPECTOR`) and the *Label*.
. Check *Own records only* if the role must only see the records the person created.
. Click *Create*. Then grant its permissions (<<admin-permissions>>) and assign it to people.

.Special cases
* `ADMIN` cannot be deleted.
* *Own records only* applies when every role of the person has it; if they also have a role without that mark,
  they see everything that other role allows.
* Role names are used in workflows (reserved transitions) and in notifications.

[[admin-permissions]]
=== Permissions

menu:Administration[Permissions] edits what a role can do (<<admin-fig-permissions>>).

:admin-permissions-alt: Permission matrix with READ checked on the three objects and field permissions of \
Contribuyente with DNI/RUC and Teléfono without read
[[admin-fig-permissions]]
.Permission matrix and field permissions of the Consulta role
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/08-permissions.png["{admin-permissions-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Objective
Grant actions to a role, or withdraw them, object by object, and restrict fields.

.Procedure
. Pick the *Role*.
. In *Permission matrix*, check per object `READ`, `CREATE`, `UPDATE` and `DELETE`, and the *Own actions* the
  object declares. The *Every object* row grants the action on any object, present or future; only that row has
  `MANAGE_METADATA` and `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION`.
. Click *Save permissions*.
. To restrict fields, in *Field permissions* pick an object and uncheck *Read* or *Write* for each field. Click
  *Save field permissions*.

.Expected result
The role list reflects the new number of permissions and restricted fields.

.Verification
Sign in with a test account with that role and check what it sees and what it can save. The audit log records the
change as `admin:permission` (<<admin-audit>>).

.Special cases
* Field permissions are a restriction, not a grant: an object with no field rules for a role shows all its fields
  to that role.
* Editing the matrix of the `ADMIN` role changes nothing: `ADMIN` skips the checks.
* Saving replaces the whole set of the role.
* The changes apply from each person's next operation; the interface may need a reload to reflect them.

CAUTION: The interface does not hide screens or buttons according to permissions: the server rejects them
(_User manual_, chapter _What changes with your role_). Explain to people with read-only roles that they will see
buttons they cannot use.

[[admin-service-accounts]]
=== Service accounts

A service account identifies another system (for example, the revenue system) that calls the API with a client id
and a secret. It has roles like a person, but it never administers the organization.

:admin-service-accounts-alt: New service account form and the account integracion-rentas with its client id, role \
CONSULTA and status Enabled
[[admin-fig-service-accounts]]
.Service accounts
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/09-service-accounts.png["{admin-service-accounts-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Procedure
. In menu:Administration[Service accounts], type the *Name* (lower case, digits, `_` or `-`; it cannot be changed
  later) and check the *Roles* (`ADMIN` is not offered).
. Click *Create*.
. Copy the *Secret* shown and hand it to the owner of the system through a secure channel. Click *I saved it*.

.Expected result
The account appears *Enabled*, with its *Client id*. The system gets credentials with `POST /api/auth/token`
(`clientId`, `clientSecret`), valid for 15 minutes by default.

CAUTION: The secret is only shown once; the server stores only its fingerprint. If it is lost, rotate it.

Row actions::
Edit roles; *Disable* / *Enable* (when disabled it gets no credentials); *Rotate secret* (the previous one stops
serving to request credentials; those already issued stay valid until they expire, unless revocation is on);
delete.

WARNING: Deleting a service account makes its audit entries lose the name of the account. Disabling it keeps it.
