[[admin-app-builder]]
== Configurable screens (App Builder)

The *App Builder* section of the menu configures how the data is shown, without programming. All its screens start
the same way: you pick the *Object* in a selector and its configuration appears. Common requirement:
`MANAGE_METADATA` on the object. Reading the configuration only needs `READ`.

[[admin-views]]
=== Views

A view is a saved way of listing an object. menu:App Builder[Views] (<<admin-fig-views>>).

:admin-views-alt: View builder: technical name, label, records per page, Default view checkbox, list of columns with \
arrows to reorder them, filters and sort
[[admin-fig-views]]
.View builder with the view "Bandeja de licencias"
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/11-views-builder.png["{admin-views-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Procedure
. Pick the *Object* and, in *View*, an existing one or the option to create a new one.
. Fill in *Technical name* (lower case), *Label* and *Records per page* (1 to 200).
. Check *Default view* if the list must open with it (it unchecks the previous default).
. In *Columns*, add fields with *Add column* and order them with the arrows.
. In *Filters*, add equality conditions (_field = value_).
. In *Sort*, pick the *Field* and the *Direction*.
. Click *Save*.

.Verification
Open the list of the object: the view selector offers the new view (_User manual_, _Using a saved view_).

*Reset to default* deletes the saved view; with no views, the object uses a generated view with its first eight
visible fields.

[[admin-forms]]
=== Forms

A form arranges the editable fields of an object into titled sections. menu:App Builder[Forms]
(<<admin-fig-forms>>).

:admin-forms-alt: Form builder with the sections Identificación, Local and Evaluación and the fields of each one
[[admin-fig-forms]]
.Form builder with the form "Solicitud de licencia"
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/12-forms-builder.png["{admin-forms-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Procedure
. Pick the *Object* and the *Form* (or create one).
. Click *Add section*, type its *Section title* and add fields with *Add field*. Order sections and fields with the
  arrows.
. Click *Save*.

.Special cases
* A field can only be in one section, and only editable fields.
* A form is shown on a detail page through a *Form* component (<<admin-pages>>). Creating a new record uses the
  generated form (all editable fields), not the saved one.

[[admin-pages]]
=== Detail pages

menu:App Builder[Pages] composes the detail page of an object by dragging components (<<admin-fig-pages>>).

:admin-pages-alt: Page builder: template Main and right sidebar, component palette, canvas with tabs and properties
[[admin-fig-pages]]
.Page builder with the licence page
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/13-pages-builder.png["{admin-pages-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

The screen has three columns:

Components::
The palette. *Containers*: *Tabs*, *Tab* and *Section*. *Content*: *Form*, *Dynamic form*, *Related list*,
*History*, *Text*, *Map* (with GIS), *Workflow* (state and transitions) and *Action* (a button that applies a
transition or navigates).
Canvas::
The regions of the *Template* (for example, _MAIN_ and _RIGHT_). Drag components into them.
Properties::
The data of the selected component: title, fields or form it shows, relationship of a list, text of a note,
transition of an action, key of a tab…

.Procedure
. Pick the *Object*. If it has no saved page, the generated one is shown.
. Click *Change* to pick a template (nine available, from one to several regions).
. Drag components from the palette onto the canvas and configure them in *Properties*.
. Click *Save*.

.Special cases
* The first *Form* of the page is the one that saves; the others are shown read-only.
* The page accepts up to 200 components and 12 levels of nesting.
* *Reset to default* deletes the saved page and goes back to the generated one.

[[admin-documents]]
=== Document types

menu:App Builder[Documents] drafts the templates a record can issue (<<admin-fig-documents>>).

:admin-documents-alt: Editor of the type Certificado de licencia: name, label, prefix CLF and the text of the template \
with field placeholders
[[admin-fig-documents]]
.Template of the licence certificate
image::screenshots/{lang}/admin/14-document-types.png["{admin-documents-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

.Procedure
. Pick the *Object*. In *Types*, click *+* to create one or pick an existing one.
. Fill in *Name* (lower case), *Label* and *Sigla* (upper case letters and digits; it is the prefix of the number,
  such as `CLF` in `CLF-2026-001`).
. Draft the template. The toolbar offers *Bold*, *Italic*, *Heading* and *List*, and three insertions: *Field* (a
  field of the record), *Value* (date, date and time, user, record id, name, prefix, serial or number of the
  document) and *Table* (the records of a relationship).
. Click *Save*.

.Special cases
* The prefix cannot be repeated among the types of the organization: each type numbers on its own, per year.
* A type that has already issued documents cannot be deleted.
* Editing a template does not change the documents already issued, which keep the template and the values of their
  issue.
* To issue automatically when a state is reached, use a rule with the action _Issue a document_ (<<admin-rules>>).
