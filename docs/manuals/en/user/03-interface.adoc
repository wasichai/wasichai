=== Screen layout

The interface has two areas:

Side menu (left)::
Dark background, always visible. At the top, the name of the application. Below, the menu sections. At the foot,
your name and your email, the *Theme* selector, the language button and *Sign out*.
Workspace (right)::
Each screen has a header with its title, a line of description and, on the right, the main actions (for example,
*New record*). Below comes the content: tables, forms or cards.

=== Menu sections

The menu groups the screens by topic. Which ones appear depends on the modules installed in your application:

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Section |Entries |What it is for
|(no title) |Home |Summary and a direct shortcut to each object.
|Data |Objects, Records, Relationships |*Objects* lists the kinds of information and leads to their records. *Records*
is always shown disabled: records are reached from an object. *Relationships* is an administration screen.
|GIS |Maps, Layers, Map views |*Maps* shows the geometries of an object. *Layers* is for administration.
*Map views* lists the saved maps, each with its object, geometry and style.
|App Builder |Pages, Forms, Documents, Views |Configuration tools for the administrator.
|Automation |Workflows, Rules, Runs |Configuration of workflows and rules; *Runs* shows what the rules did.
|Administration |Users, Roles, Service accounts, Permissions, Audit |Management of accounts and permissions, and the
audit log.
|===

IMPORTANT: The menu shows every section to anyone who has signed in, whether or not they have permission to use
it. If you open a screen you have no permission for, the screen appears empty or the server rejects the action with
a message (<<user-roles>>). An entry being in the menu does not mean you can use it.

=== The Home page

*Home* shows how many objects there are in the organization and, if the application has the GIS module, how many
have geometry. Below there is one card per object; click a card to open the list of its records. Under the name of
each object it says whether it has geometry (for example, `POLYGON · EPSG:4326`) or *No geometry*.

Only the objects you can read appear.

[[user-preferences]]
=== Theme and language

.Objective
Change the look (light or dark) or the language of the interface.

.Procedure
. For the theme, pick an option in the *Theme* selector at the foot of the menu: *System* (follows the setting of
  your computer), *Light* or *Dark*. Your application may offer its own themes.
. For the language, click the button with the code of the other language (*ES* when the interface is in English,
  *EN* when it is in Spanish).

.Expected result
The interface changes at once, without reloading the page.

.Verification
Sign out and sign in again, or sign in from another computer: the chosen theme and language are kept.

.Special cases
* The preference is stored on the server, tied to your account, and so it follows you to any computer.
* The language changes the texts of the interface, not the data or the names the administrator wrote. Some
  messages the server sends are always in English (<<user-messages>>).
