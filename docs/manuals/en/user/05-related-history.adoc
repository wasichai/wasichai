== Related records and history

[[user-related]]
=== Seeing the related records

A record is linked to others through relationships: a licence has a _Predio_ and a _Solicitante_; a taxpayer has
their _Predios_ and their _Licencias_. The detail page shows these links in related lists (<<user-fig-related>>).

.Procedure
. Open the record.
. Go to the tab that holds the related lists: in the example, *Predio y solicitante*; in a generated page,
  *Related*.

:user-related-alt: Predio y solicitante tab with two tables: the plot P-0004 and the applicant C-0012, each with its \
Edit link
[[user-fig-related]]
.Related lists of a licence
image::screenshots/{lang}/user/10-record-related.png["{user-related-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

Each list shows the name of the relationship, its type (for example, `MANY_TO_ONE`) and how many records it holds.
*Edit* opens the related record. The lists work both ways: from a taxpayer you see their licences, from a licence,
its applicant.

NOTE: To change the applicant or the plot of a licence, change the matching field in the form and save.
Many-to-many relationships are managed from the related list.

[[user-history]]
=== Looking at the history of a record

Every creation, change, deletion, transition and document issue is recorded in the history of the record, which
cannot be changed or deleted from the application.

.Procedure
. Open the record and go to the *Historial* tab.

:user-history-alt: Historial tab with two entries: Create and Update, each with user, relative date, reason and the \
change of state from borrador to en_revision
[[user-fig-history]]
.History of a licence
image::screenshots/{lang}/user/11-record-history.png["{user-history-alt}",pdfwidth=100%]

Each entry shows:

* The type of operation: *Create*, *Update*, *Delete* or *Issue* (of a document).
* Who did it: the email of the user, or *System* when an automatic rule did it.
* When, in relative time (_9 minutes ago_).
* The *Reason*, if one was given.
* The fields that changed, with the previous value and the new one. A change of workflow state appears as `state`
  with the technical names of the states (for example, `borrador → en_revision`).

NOTE: The history respects field permissions: if your role cannot read a field, its changes do not appear in the
history either.
