[[user-faq]]
== Frequently asked questions

Can I recover a record I deleted?::
Not from the application. Deletion is final; the audit log keeps that it existed and who deleted it. Only a
technical restore of a backup could recover it, and it would affect the whole database.

Why can I not change the state of a record from the form?::
Because the state is not a field: it only changes through the transitions of the workflow (<<user-workflow>>),
which check your role and are recorded.

Why does a column show a long code such as `a8a1e643-…`?::
It is the internal identifier of the related record. Open the record to see the link by its code, or ask the
administrator for a view without that column.

Can I export a list to Excel or CSV?::
The interface has no export. A technical administrator can extract the data through the API.

Why do I see screens in the menu that I cannot use?::
The menu is the same for everyone. What you can do is decided by the server on every operation (<<user-roles>>).

Why does a document not reflect a change I made to the record?::
Documents are frozen when issued. Issue a new one of the same type: the previous one becomes *Archived*.

Why does the map not show streets?::
The base map comes from an external service. If your network does not allow access to it, only the geometries are
seen.

Are my changes saved on their own?::
No. Click *Save*. If the session expires first, what you typed is lost.

Who sees what I do?::
Every change stays in the history of the record and in the audit log of the organization, with your user, the time
and the reason.
