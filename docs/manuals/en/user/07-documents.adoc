[[user-documents]]
== Issuing and printing documents

A *document* is a numbered piece of writing generated from a record using an administrator's template: a
certificate, a resolution, an official letter. When it is issued it gets a sequential number (for example,
`CLF-2026-001`: prefix, year and sequence) and is *frozen*: it keeps the template and the values as they were at
that moment. Changing the record or the template afterwards does not alter a document already issued.

[[user-issue-document]]
=== Issuing a document

.Objective
Generate a numbered document from a record.

.Prerequisites
* Read permission on the object.
* The administrator has created at least one document type for the object.

.Procedure
. Open the record and scroll down to the *Issued documents* panel, at the foot of the page.
. In *Pick a type*, pick the document type.
. Click *Issue*.

.Expected result
The document appears in the table of the panel with its *Number*, the date and time it was *Issued* and the
*Status* *Valid* (<<user-fig-documents>>).

[[user-fig-documents]]
.Issued documents panel of an approved licence
image::screenshots/{lang}/user/12-record-documents.png[Foot of the page of a licence with the form and the Issued documents panel with the valid certificate CLF-2026-001,pdfwidth=100%]

.Verification
The history of the record shows an *Issue* entry.

.Special cases
* Issuing a document of the same type again for the same record archives the previous one: there is always at most
  one *Valid* per type and record; the others stay as *Archived*.
* Some documents are issued on their own when a workflow state is reached. In the example, the certificate is
  issued when the licence is approved; in the history that issue appears under *System*.
* A document shows all its data to whoever can see it: field permissions do not apply to it, unlike the rest of
  the application.

[[user-print-document]]
=== Viewing and printing a document

.Procedure
. In the *Issued documents* panel, click the row of the document. A dialog opens with the document and the *Print*
  link.
. Click *Print*. The printable sheet opens in a new tab (<<user-fig-print>>).
. Click *Print / Save PDF* and pick the printer, or _Save as PDF_ in the print dialog of the browser.

NOTE: The printable sheet is always shown with the light theme, whatever theme was chosen. An archived document is
