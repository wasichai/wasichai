== What changes with your role

What you can do is decided by the roles the administrator assigned to your account. Each role grants, object by
object, four actions on the records: read (`READ`), create (`CREATE`), update (`UPDATE`) and delete (`DELETE`). It
can also hide specific fields and limit the person to the records they created themselves, and the transitions of a
workflow can be reserved to certain roles.

=== How it shows on screen

The server always does the check, on every operation. The interface hides some things, but not all:

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |What you see
|You cannot read an object |The object does not appear in *Home* or in *Objects*.
|You cannot read a field |The field does not appear in the form, in the list or in the history.
|You can read but not update |The form is shown and the *Save* button is available, but on saving the server
answers *Missing permission UPDATE* and nothing changes (<<user-fig-forbidden>>).
|The transition belongs to another role |The button appears disabled with the reason, for example
*requires role REVISOR*.
|Administration screens |They are visible in the menu. Without the matching permission, the server rejects your
actions.
|===

=== Example: three profiles of the Distrito Demo

Registrador (_Rosa Registradora_)::
Reads, creates and updates licences and taxpayers; only reads plots. Can send a licence to review and correct
remarks, but cannot approve or reject.
Revisor técnico (_Raúl Revisor_)::
Reads the three objects and updates licences. Can make remarks on, approve and reject licences in review.
Consulta (_Carmen Consulta_)::
Read only. Besides, does not see the identity document or the phone of taxpayers (<<user-fig-field-permissions>>).

TIP: If you need to do something your role does not allow, ask the administrator to review your roles. Do not share
your account: everything done with it is recorded in your name in the audit log.
